❌ The Problem

Garbage collection companies manage hundreds of households using paper and Excel. Here is what breaks:

📋

Paper Records

Hundreds of households tracked in notebooks. No search, no backup, no history.

💸

Revenue Leakage

M-PESA payments get lost between the phone and the books. No one knows who paid.

📵

No Reminders

Residents forget to pay. No SMS alerts = late payments = cash flow problems.

🚛

Blind Operations

Manager has zero visibility. Which houses were collected? Who owes what?

✅ The Solution

Smart Bin automates the entire garbage collection lifecycle from estate setup to payment reconciliation.

1

Estate & House Setup

Create an estate → phases auto-generate → houses get account numbers like 348/4 automatically via MySQL trigger.

2

Automated Monthly Billing

One API call generates bills for ALL houses in an estate. No manual spreadsheets. No missed houses.

3

M-PESA Auto-Reconciliation

Resident pays via Paybill → system validates account → records payment → updates balance → all automatic.

4

SMS Notifications

Bill issued → SMS sent. Payment received → confirmation SMS sent instantly. Overdue? Bulk reminder.

5

Real-Time Dashboard

Revenue, collections, overdue aging, driver performance — everything visible in one place.
